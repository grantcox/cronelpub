# The Blue Angel Statue

EVERY MONTH in Santa Barbara we have a thing called “First Thursday.”  During the evening of the first Thursday of the month, all of the art galleries stay open, plus some bars, restaurants, museums and government buildings have art displays.  Usually, each place has free snacks and some have free wine or wine you have to pay for.  One time, there was a lot of free wine and after a while I realized that I should not operate a motor vehicle in my condition.  I got a large fries at McDonald’s and walked around for an hour and a half.  Then I drove home on side streets to be extra safe.  A week later, I found myself still thinking about the blue angel statue that I had seen.  After a week, it could not be the alcohol talking, so I drove to the art gallery and bought the statue.
