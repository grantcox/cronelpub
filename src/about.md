Steven Chavin is a retired physicist who has spent a lifetime asking how the
world works — and why it sometimes doesn't seem to.

Raised in Chicago and now living in Santa Barbara, he draws on personal
memories, curiosity, and a deep interest in the nature of thought and belief.
When he's not writing, he gives lively tours as a courthouse docent.

*Sleep in Heavenly Peace* is his first novel, blending philosophy, memory, and
imagination.
