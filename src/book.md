Stuart's earliest memories don't behave the way they should. Moments sharpen
and vanish. Fear brings sudden clarity, then disappears just as quickly. Even
as a child, he senses patterns — repetitions he can't explain and can't hold
onto long enough to understand.

As he grows, the world doesn't settle into something predictable. Instead, it
becomes more unsettling. Ordinary experiences take on strange weight, and the
line between what is seen and what is felt begins to blur.

And somewhere beyond his understanding, something is watching.

When Stuart begins to sense a presence guiding him — subtle, unseen, but
undeniably real — his questions take on new urgency. Because if he is not
alone… then something far greater is at work. And when danger comes, the
boundary between his world and something beyond it may be the only thing that
can save him.

*Sleep in Heavenly Peace* is a philosophical mystery that explores memory,
perception, and the possibility that we are never as alone — or as in
control — as we believe.
