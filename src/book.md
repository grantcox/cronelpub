As Stuart grows, the line between what is seen and what is felt begins to blur.  And somewhere beyond his understanding, something or someone is watching.  In addition to life’s normal problems, Stuart is forced to admit that he possesses abilities that no one else possesses.  Some of his abilities burdens him with a moral question. 

Stuart senses this presence guiding him—subtle, unseen, but undeniably real.  He is not alone and something out there is at work.  And when danger comes, help from the place that lies beyond his world may be the only thing that can save him.

_Sleep in Heavenly Peace_ is a philosophical mystery that explores memory,
perception, and the possibility that we are never as alone — or as in
control — as we believe.
